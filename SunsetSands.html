Best of DownSouth

See All

Galle Fort

Galle Fort is one of Sri Lanka’s most iconic historical landmarks, located along the southern coast. Built in the 16th century, wander massive stone walls, charming cafés, and Indian Ocean views.

Hikkaduwa Turtle Beach

Hikkaduwa Turtle Beach offers visitors a chance to see beautiful sea turtles in their natural environment. Enjoy swimming, snorkeling, and watching gentle marine creatures close to the shore.

Turtle Hatchery & Rescue

Visit the Sea Turtle Hatchery & Rescue Center in Hikkaduwa to experience vital marine conservation. Meet rescued giant sea turtles and see baby hatchlings prepared for ocean release.
