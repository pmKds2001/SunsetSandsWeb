Discover the Historic Galle Fort: A Journey Through Sri Lanka's Living Fortress

Built by the Portuguese in the 16th century and expanded by the Dutch, Galle Fort is surrounded by massive stone ramparts that have stood for centuries. Wander cobblestone streets, colonial-era architecture, historic churches, boutique cafés, and panoramic views of the Indian Ocean.
